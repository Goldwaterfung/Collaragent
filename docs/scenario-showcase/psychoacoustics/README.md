# Psychoacoustics: End-to-End Research Showcase

This showcase documents a complete, 6-stage end-to-end scientific research workflow in **Psychoacoustics & Spatial Audio Perception** (cross-domain: Acoustic Signal Processing + Cognitive Psychology), executed collaboratively between a human researcher and DeepAgent in CollarAgent.

## Research Focus

- **Topic**: Motion-to-Sound Perceptual Latency Thresholds and Spatial Localization in 6-DoF Binaural Audio.
- **Theoretical Foundations**:
  - Rayleigh's Duplex Theory of Sound Localization (Interaural Time Differences [ITD] & Interaural Level Differences [ILD]).
  - Head-Related Transfer Functions (HRTF) and Spectral Pinna Cues (resolving the Cone of Confusion).
  - Levitt's Transformed Up-Down Adaptive Staircase Method (Psychophysical 2AFC Paradigms).
  - Cross-Modal Binding and the Ventriloquism Effect in Multimodal Virtual Environments.
- **Core Investigation**: How rotational head velocity interacts with audiovisual spatial anchoring to modulate the Just Noticeable Difference (JND) detection threshold for latency in dynamic 6-DoF spatial audio rendering.
